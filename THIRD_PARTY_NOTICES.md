## Exam materials

The task statements, data files, figures, answer keys and the original PDF sheets come from the
matura exams in computer science (informatyka, poziom rozszerzony, formuła 2023) published by the
Centralna Komisja Egzaminacyjna (CKE, [cke.gov.pl](https://cke.gov.pl/)). They were downloaded
from [arkusze.pl](https://arkusze.pl/). All rights to these materials belong to CKE. They are
included unchanged in meaning, for exam practice only. The PDFs in `content/pdf/` are the
originals.

These exams are included:

- May 2023 and June 2023
- May 2024 and June 2024
- May 2025 and June 2025
- May 2026 and June 2026
